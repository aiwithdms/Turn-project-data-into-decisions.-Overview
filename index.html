Decision-making checklist

1
State the observation

Use a number, comparison or visible pattern.

2
Check the context

Confirm dates, scope, units and data source.

3
Test possible causes

Look for corroborating evidence before concluding.

4
Recommend a next step

Make it specific, feasible and measurable.

You can have a correct calculation and still make a poor recommendation if you assume a cause the data does not prove.

AI-assisted analysis: safe prompt pattern

Use only with approved tools and permitted, anonymized data.

“Review this approved, anonymized project summary. Separate observed facts from hypotheses. Identify the three largest variances, suggest evidence to verify possible causes, and draft a concise management summary. Do not invent missing data or claim causation without evidence.”
